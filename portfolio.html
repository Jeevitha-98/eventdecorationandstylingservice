Our Identity

The Creative Mindset Behind Stackly

Born out of a deep-seated passion for transforming spaces, Stackly is a collective of event designers, floral artisans, and lighting technicians based in Coimbatore. We don’t believe in cookie-cutter templates—we believe every celebration deserves a unique visual landscape.

Our approach blends traditional cultural roots with clean modern geometry. From local micro-functions to grand luxury destination weddings, we treat every layout vector as a pristine canvas to render your milestone story beautifully.
